        How we know

        Sunrise and sunset are worked out on this page from the date and town (sun-position formulae, shown in Irish time, so summer time is handled). The solstice and equinox dates come from the U.S. Naval Observatory's published tables. The quarter days are the traditional fixed dates. Nothing is sent anywhere.
